THE IDEA BEHIND QONYS

Comfort begins with a place to belong

A student project about homes, new beginnings and everyday comfort.

OUR STORY

A home is part of your everyday life

Moving to a new city, starting university or beginning a new job can be exciting. A comfortable home makes these changes a little easier.

We created QONYS to bring apartment photos, prices and useful details together. We wanted visitors to explore different spaces without getting lost in unnecessary information.

Our collection uses sample apartments in Almaty and Astana to show how a simple rental website can help people compare their options.

WHAT MATTERS TO US

Simple ideas behind a welcoming home

01

Comfort

A home should offer space to rest, study, work and enjoy ordinary moments.

02

Simplicity

Photos, room sizes and prices should be easy to find and understand.

03

Clarity

Monthly rent, deposits and additional costs should be shown separately.

A SPACE FOR YOUR ROUTINE

Choose around the way you live

A good choice is about more than the number of rooms. Think about the small things that make your day easier.

  1. Decide which city and area work for you.
  2. Think about your space and furniture needs.
  3. Compare the full cost with your budget.
  4. Prepare questions before viewing a real property.
