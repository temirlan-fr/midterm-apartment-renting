GOOD TO KNOW

Before you choose your new space

A few answers to help you understand the sample listings.

What is included in the rent?

The sample rent includes the apartment, furniture and kitchen appliances. Utilities, internet and parking are listed separately.

Why is there a separate deposit?

A deposit is an additional upfront amount. In our examples, it equals one month's rent. For a real rental, ask about the return conditions before agreeing.

Where can I compare the prices?

The Prices page shows the city, number of rooms, monthly rent and deposit for every sample apartment.

Can I make a real booking here?

No. QONYS is a student project. You can explore the apartments and try the form, but the website does not create bookings.
